# Remote Work and Team Productivity

## Introduction

In today's rapidly evolving workplace, remote work plays a pivotal role in shaping how teams collaborate. Moreover, it underscores the need for flexible tools. Furthermore, it is worth noting that organisations must navigate the complexities of distributed communication. In conclusion, remote work paves the way for a more seamless future of work.

## What we measured

We tracked 42 engineers across three time zones from March to June 2025. Median pull-request review time fell from 19 hours to 11 hours after we moved stand-ups to a written format, while the number of reopened tickets stayed flat at about 6% per sprint.

## Discussion

Remote work not only improves flexibility but also enhances employee well-being, fosters innovation and drives long-term organisational success. These multifaceted benefits highlight its significant implications for the modern enterprise.

Two caveats limit what we can claim. The team was already experienced with asynchronous tools, and the period overlapped with a hiring freeze, so review load per person dropped by roughly a fifth.
